## T-10 Days — Campaign Kickoff

### Brief & Planning
- [ ] Campaign brief received and reviewed — objectives, concept, budget, and timeline confirmed
- [ ] Campaign requirements document prepared — scope, scale, vendor categories, product volumes
- [ ] Site confirmed and venue agreement signed
- [ ] Master campaign timeline drafted and shared with client

### Site
- [ ] First site inspection completed — access points, power, water, layout, and crowd flow assessed
- [ ] Site layout map drafted — stage, food stalls, games zones, entry gates, signage positions
- [ ] Infrastructure requirements identified — power, generators, lighting, waste management

### Permits & Compliance
- [ ] Local authority permit applications submitted
- [ ] Entertainment and public event licences applied for
- [ ] Health and safety compliance requirements identified

---

## T-8 Days — Vendor Mobilisation

### Vendor Sourcing
- [ ] All vendor categories identified and shortlisted — food stalls, AV, fabrication, logistics, staffing, security, photography, generators, signage
- [ ] Minimum three quotes obtained for key service categories
- [ ] Vendor selection completed and confirmed across all categories
- [ ] All vendor contracts and agreements signed

### Vendor Briefing
- [ ] Individual vendor briefing documents prepared for all 20+ vendors
- [ ] Scope of work, schedule, site access, and compliance requirements communicated to each vendor
- [ ] Vendor contact list compiled and distributed to operations team
- [ ] Vendor register updated with all confirmed vendors for this campaign

### Product
- [ ] Product volume forecast completed — entry gate quantities, food stall allocations, buffer stock calculated
- [ ] Product procurement order placed and confirmed with supplier
- [ ] Storage facility confirmed and ready — cold storage capacity verified
- [ ] Product intake and inventory management plan prepared

---

## T-6 Days — Infrastructure & Logistics Planning

### Site & Infrastructure
- [ ] Second site inspection completed — confirm layout against vendor setup requirements
- [ ] Power and generator plan finalised — load requirements mapped against equipment list
- [ ] Lighting plan confirmed for evening and night operations
- [ ] Waste management and cleaning plan confirmed
- [ ] Security perimeter plan finalised — entry gates, exit points, crowd control positions

### Logistics
- [ ] Product delivery schedule confirmed — intake date, quantities, storage allocation
- [ ] Event day product distribution plan prepared — quantities per zone, replenishment cycle schedule
- [ ] Transport and vehicle plan confirmed for event day logistics
- [ ] Replenishment team assigned and briefed

### Permits & Compliance
- [ ] All permits and licences received and filed
- [ ] Site safety requirements communicated to all vendors
- [ ] First aid and emergency response plan confirmed

---

## T-4 Days — Final Confirmations

### Vendors
- [ ] All vendor arrival times and setup windows confirmed in writing
- [ ] Master vendor schedule finalised and distributed to all team members
- [ ] Inter-vendor dependencies mapped and communicated to relevant vendors
- [ ] Any vendor substitutions or last-minute changes resolved and documented

### Product
- [ ] Product stock received — intake inspection completed
- [ ] All stock logged into inventory system — quantities, batch details, storage zone
- [ ] FIFO rotation applied — stock organised for dispatch sequence
- [ ] Entry gate product quantities pre-packed and labelled ready for event day deployment

### Staffing
- [ ] Promotional staff headcount confirmed
- [ ] Staff roles and positions assigned — entry gates, food stall support, crowd flow, operations
- [ ] Staff briefing session scheduled for T-1

### Communications
- [ ] Event day emergency contact list finalised and distributed
- [ ] Client representative contact confirmed
- [ ] All team members have copies of master vendor schedule and run sheet

---

## T-2 Days — Final Preparations

### Site
- [ ] Final site inspection completed — all infrastructure, access, and zone readiness confirmed
- [ ] Any outstanding site issues resolved or escalation plan in place
- [ ] Signage and branding installation plan confirmed with vendor

### Vendors
- [ ] Final confirmation calls made to all 20+ vendors — arrival time, requirements, and any last updates
- [ ] Any outstanding vendor issues flagged and resolved
- [ ] Pack-down plan confirmed with all vendors

### Product
- [ ] Final stock count completed — quantities confirmed against event day plan
- [ ] Any stock shortfalls identified and addressed
- [ ] Replenishment stock staged and ready for event day movement

### Logistics
- [ ] All vehicles and transport confirmed for event day
- [ ] Driver briefings completed — routes, timing, on-site access, and contacts
- [ ] Cold-chain transport arrangements confirmed where applicable

---

## T-1 Day — Final Briefings & Sign-Offs

### Team Briefing
- [ ] Full team briefing conducted — all staff and key vendor contacts present
- [ ] Event day run sheet distributed and walked through
- [ ] Roles, responsibilities, and escalation contacts confirmed with every team member
- [ ] Q&A completed — all outstanding questions resolved

### Operations
- [ ] Event day run sheet finalised and printed for all team leads
- [ ] On-day issue log template prepared and distributed
- [ ] Replenishment cycle plan confirmed with logistics team
- [ ] All communication channels tested — phones, radios if applicable

### Final Checks
- [ ] Weather conditions reviewed — contingency plan confirmed if applicable
- [ ] Client final confirmation received
- [ ] Operations coordinator personal checklist reviewed — nothing outstanding

---

- [ ] Operations coordinator on-site by 05:30
- [ ] Vendor arrival monitoring begins at 06:00
- [ ] Setup sign-off walkthrough completed by 13:30
- [ ] Gates open at 14:00 — all zones operational
- [ ] Refer to [Event Day Run Sheet](./event-day-run-sheet.md) for full hour-by-hour schedule

## Post-Event

- [ ] Stock reconciliation completed within 24 hours
- [ ] Issue log finalised within 24 hours
- [ ] Vendor assessments completed within 48 hours
- [ ] Post-event report submitted to client within 72 hours
- [ ] Vendor register updated with performance notes
- [ ] Learnings documented for future campaign planning
